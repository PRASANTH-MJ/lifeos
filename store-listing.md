## Full description (4000 char max)
```
LifeOS is a single app for the everyday habits and routines that make up a
life — without handing your personal data to anyone.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Habit Tracker — yes/no, numeric, timer, or checklist habits, with streaks,
  custom frequencies, and a full history calendar
• Task Management — one-time and recurring to-dos, priorities, reminders,
  and alarms
• Calendar — a unified view of every habit, task, and event due each day
• Meditation & Breathing — guided sessions and paced breathing exercises
• Mind Training — quick reaction-time, memory, and focus mini-games
• Workout Suggestions — a recommendation that adapts to your equipment and
  time available
• Affirmations — a new one every day, plus your own favorites
• Finance Tracker — log income and expenses, see where it's going by category
• Journal — a private daily journal with mood tracking
• Food Tracker — log meals and calories by day
• Insights Dashboard — trends across every module in one place

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
Everything you create — every habit, task, journal entry, mood, meal,
workout — is stored locally on your device, never uploaded anywhere. The
only thing sent to a server is what's needed to sign you in. No ads, no
analytics, no data selling.

Simple, private, and built to actually be used every day.
```

## Category
**Recommendation: Health & Fitness** (primary emphasis on habits/wellness/meditation/journaling). **Productivity** is a reasonable alternative given the task/calendar side — Play Console only allows one primary category, so pick whichever you want the app discovered under.

## Content rating questionnaire (heads-up, not text to paste)
No violence, no user-generated public content, no gambling, no ads. Should land as "Everyone" / "PEGI 3" once you fill in Play Console's questionnaire honestly.
