LifeOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-23

LifeOS ("the app") is a personal habit, task, and wellness tracker. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Account data

To use the app you create an account with an email address, a username, and a password. Your password is hashed (never stored in plain text) before being saved. This account data is used solely to let you sign in — it is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Everything else stays on your device

Habits, tasks, journal entries, mood logs, meditation/breathing sessions, finance entries, food logs, workout data, and every other piece of content you create in the app is stored locally on your device only. None of it is transmitted to, or stored on, any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently — there is no cloud backup or sync.

Notifications

If you enable reminders or alarms, the app schedules local notifications on your device using your device's operating system. No notification content is sent through a third-party push service.

Data retention and deletion

Account data (email, username, hashed password) is retained until you ask for it to be deleted. To request deletion of your account, contact the developer using the details below.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the app's Play Store page.
